📘 Report: Cross-Platform Prompting – Evaluating Diverse Techniques in AI-Powered Text Summarization
1. Aim
•	Objective: To understand and evaluate different prompting techniques across multiple AI platforms (ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot) by performing text summarization and comparing outputs.
________________________________________
2. Experiment
•	Task: Summarize a common input text (e.g., Blockchain Technology) using multiple prompting techniques.
•	Platforms Used: ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot.
•	Techniques Applied: 
1.	Zero-Shot Prompting – Summarize without examples.
2.	Few-Shot Prompting – Summarize with 2–3 examples.
3.	Chain-of-Thought Prompting – Generate step-by-step reasoning before summarization.
4.	Role-Based Prompting – Summarize from a perspective (e.g., researcher, student).
________________________________________
3. Algorithm
1.	Select a common paragraph for summarization.
2.	Provide identical input text to all platforms.
3.	Apply each prompting technique systematically.
4.	Record outputs.
5.	Compare outputs based on evaluation criteria.
6.	Prepare a comparison table.
7.	Document observations and conclusion.
________________________________________
4. Evaluation Criteria
•	Accuracy – Correctness of content.
•	Coherence – Logical flow.
•	Simplicity – Ease of understanding.
•	Speed – Response time.
•	User Experience – Ease of use.
________________________________________
5. Comparison Table (Illustrative Example)
Prompting Technique	ChatGPT	Gemini	Copilot	Accuracy	Coherence	Simplicity	Speed	UX
Zero-Shot	Concise	Slightly vague	Clear	High	Medium	High	Fast	Good
Few-Shot	Detailed	Structured	Balanced	High	High	Medium	Moderate	Good
Chain-of-Thought	Stepwise	Overly verbose	Logical	Very High	High	Medium	Slower	Moderate
Role-Based	Researcher tone	Student tone	Analyst tone	High	High	High	Fast	Excellent
________________________________________
6. Observations
•	Zero-Shot: Quick and simple, but sometimes lacks depth.
•	Few-Shot: Improves clarity and accuracy with examples.
•	Chain-of-Thought: Best for detailed reasoning but slower.
•	Role-Based: Highly adaptable, improves user engagement.
•	 
________________________________________
7. Result
•	Different prompting techniques yield varied outputs across platforms.
•	Few-Shot and Role-Based prompting provided the most balanced results in terms of accuracy and user experience.
•	Chain-of-Thought was strongest for analytical depth but slower.
