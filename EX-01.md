Introduction
Prompt Engineering is the process of designing effective inputs (prompts) to obtain accurate, meaningful, and relevant responses from Artificial Intelligence models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot. It helps users communicate clearly with AI systems and improves the quality of generated outputs. Effective prompts provide proper context, instructions, and formatting requirements to guide AI behavior. (GitHub)
Prompt engineering plays an important role in Generative AI applications including content generation, summarization, classification, coding assistance, and conversational systems. Different prompting techniques such as zero-shot prompting, conditional prompting, and multiple-choice prompting are commonly used to improve AI performance. (Microsoft)

Theory
What is a Prompt?
A prompt is a text instruction or query given to an AI model to generate a response. It acts as a communication bridge between the user and the AI system. (SITAMS)
Example
Prompt:
“Explain the water cycle in simple terms.”
AI Output:
“The water cycle is the continuous movement of water through evaporation, condensation, and precipitation.”
________________________________________
Types of Prompt Engineering Techniques
1. Seed Text Prompt
A simple and open-ended statement used to start AI content generation.
Example
Prompt:
“The space is…”
Output:
“The space is vast, mysterious, and full of endless possibilities.”

 
________________________________________
2. Conditional Prompt
A prompt that instructs the AI to generate content under specific conditions or constraints.
Example
Prompt:
“Draft a story about a company building a spaceship to Mars.”
Output:
AI generates a story according to the given condition.
________________________________________
3. Multiple Choice Prompt
The AI selects the correct answer from multiple options.
Example
Prompt:
“What do we need to fly to Mars?
A) Chocolate
B) Bicycle
C) Spaceship
D) Car”
Output:
“C) Spaceship”
________________________________________
4. Zero-Shot Prompting
The AI performs a task without prior examples.
Example
Prompt:
“Classify the sentiment: ‘I love space exploration.’”
Output:
“Positive”
________________________________________
5. Structured Prompt
The AI is instructed to provide output in a specific format such as JSON or tables.
Example
Prompt:
“Provide the result in JSON format.”
Output:
{
  "sentiment": "Positive"
}
